HydroTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-09

This policy explains what data HydroTrack ("the app") collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Who we are

HydroTrack is developed by Videk. Contact: vipbuildapps@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you create an account (email/password) or continue as a guest (anonymous account), we store:

  • Your email address (email accounts only) — used solely for sign-in and password recovery.
  • Profile details you provide: gender, weight, height, daily water goal, preferred unit (ml/oz), and light/dark appearance preference.
  • Your water intake log: amount, drink type (water, coffee, tea, soda, juice, alcohol), and timestamp of each drink you record.
  • Your reminder schedule: times, repeat days, custom reminder text (if you write one), and whether reminders are enabled.
  • Achievement/streak progress derived from your intake log.

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or any data beyond what's listed above.

How we use it

  • To calculate a suggested daily hydration goal from your weight, height, and gender.
  • To show your progress, history, insights, and statistics back to you.
  • To schedule local reminder notifications on your device.
  • To sync your data across your own devices when signed in with an email account.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or other third parties.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored in a Supabase Postgres database. Supabase acts as our data processor; access is restricted with Row Level Security so only you can read or write your own data. Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device based on the schedule you set. We do not use push notification services and do not send you notifications from a server.

Data export

You can export your own intake history as a CSV file at any time from within the app.

Guest (anonymous) accounts

If you continue as a guest, your data is tied to an anonymous account identifier stored only on that device. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data will make that data unrecoverable, since there is no email to link it back to.

Data deletion

You can delete individual intake log entries and reminders at any time in the app. To delete your entire account and all associated data, contact vipbuildapps@gmail.com and we will remove your account and data within 30 days.

Children's privacy

HydroTrack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
