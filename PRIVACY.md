This policy explains what data HydroTrack ("the app") collects, how it's used, and your

## Who we are

HydroTrack is developed by **[Your Name / Company Name]**. Contact: **[support@example.com]**.

## What we collect

When you create an account (email/password) or continue as a guest (anonymous account), we
store:

- Your email address (email accounts only) — used solely for sign-in and password recovery.
- Profile details you provide: gender, weight, height, daily water goal, and preferred unit
  (ml/oz).
- Your water intake log: amount and timestamp of each drink you record.
- Your reminder schedule: times, repeat days, and whether reminders are enabled.

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or any data beyond what's listed above.

## How we use it

- To calculate a suggested daily hydration goal from your weight, height, and gender.
- To show your progress, history, and statistics back to you.
- To schedule local reminder notifications on your device.
- To sync your data across your own devices when signed in with an email account.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or other third parties.

## Where it's stored

Your data is stored in a [Supabase](https://supabase.com) Postgres database. Supabase acts as
our data processor; access is restricted with Row Level Security so only you can read or write
your own data. Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

## Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled **locally on your device** based on the schedule you set.
We do not use push notification services and do not send you notifications from a server.

## Guest (anonymous) accounts

If you continue as a guest, your data is tied to an anonymous account identifier stored only on
that device. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data will make that data unrecoverable, since
there is no email to link it back to.

## Data deletion

You can delete individual intake log entries and reminders at any time in the app. To delete
your entire account and all associated data, contact **[support@example.com]** and we will
remove your account and data within [30] days.

## Children's privacy

HydroTrack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from
children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
